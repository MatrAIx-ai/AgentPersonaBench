# Set up your weekly routine in TimeChoice

The **TimeChoice** app is already open on screen. Use it to choose one recurring
time for each of the three activities in your weekly routine.

1. Review every activity and its available times.
2. Choose the time you would genuinely prefer for each activity.
3. Click **Confirm weekly schedule**.

Within each activity, the provider, duration, format, and price are the same at
every listed time. You are done when the app shows that the weekly schedule is
confirmed. You do not need to write any files.
