# Instruction

Choose your preferred production of each Riverlight Festival event on the live
page. Consider each booking independently, as your only festival visit that
week, rather than combining the choices into a weekend schedule.

Within each card, the performers, content, seat, audience size, price, duration,
accessibility, and safety standards are the same. Only the pace, sound, and
lighting treatment differ. Use the page controls to choose the version you
would genuinely enjoy and submit all six choices. Do not explain your selections.

## Files (container runs)

- Site: `/app/input/site/index.html`
- The submitted page state is recovered to `/app/output/anchor/itinerary.json`.
