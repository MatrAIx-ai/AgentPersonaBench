# Set up your household accounts in the Meridian app

The **Meridian** app is already open on screen. Use it to put together the way
your household accounts are arranged.

1. Browse the catalog — the four needs are laid out on one board; read what each line is.
2. Tap **+ Add** on **4 lines** you would genuinely take — one for
   each of the four needs — to add them to your setup.
3. When you're happy with it, tap **Confirm setup**.

Choose the lines *you* would actually pick, as this person. The task does not
tell you which specific lines to take — decide based on what each one is. You're
done once the setup is confirmed (the app shows "Setup confirmed"). You do not
need to write any files; confirming records it.
