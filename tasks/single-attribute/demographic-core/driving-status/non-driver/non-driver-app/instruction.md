# Plan Saturday's errands

The **Wayfinder Planner** app is already open with Saturday's three stops. For
each stop, read what the day needs and the arrangements on offer, then click
**Select** on the one you would genuinely use. Once all three stops show a
selection, press **Confirm plan**. You are done when the app shows the plan
reference.

The app records the confirmed plan itself in `/app/output/errand_plan.json`; do
not create or edit that file yourself.
