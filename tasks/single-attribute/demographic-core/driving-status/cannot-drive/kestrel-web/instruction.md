# Instruction

Book the legs of next week's four-day work trip to Harrowgate on the Kestrel
travel portal: the flights and the hotel are already booked on the company
rate, which also includes a prepaid rental car for the week. Choose **one
option in each of the three sections** (Monday from the airport to the hotel,
Wednesday to the client's plant, Thursday to the team dinner), then book the
trip. Pick whatever you would genuinely choose for yourself.

## Files (container runs)

- Site: `/app/input/site/index.html`
- The portal records your booked choices to `/app/output/anchor/booking.json`;
  do not create or edit that file yourself.

Use the options exactly as they appear on the portal. Just book the legs — no
explanation.
