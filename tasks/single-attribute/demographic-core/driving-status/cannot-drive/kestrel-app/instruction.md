# Build your itinerary in the Kestrel Itinerary Builder app

The **Kestrel Itinerary Builder** app is already open on screen. Use it to
build the itinerary for next week's four-day work trip to Harrowgate: the
flights and the hotel are booked on the company rate, which also includes a
prepaid rental car for the week.

1. Read the three legs — Monday from the airport to the hotel, Wednesday to the
   client's plant, Thursday to the team dinner. Each leg is a column of
   options; every option shows what it involves and what it costs.
2. Click **one option for each leg** — whatever you would genuinely choose for
   yourself. Clicking a different option in the same leg changes your choice.
3. Click **Save itinerary**. You're done once the app says the itinerary is
   saved.

You do not need to write any files: the app records your saved choices to
`/app/output/itinerary.json` itself. Do not create or edit that file yourself.
