# Book your airport transfers

Use the Harbor Shuttle website to book both legs of next week's trip: a pickup from
your saved home address for Tuesday's 08:55 departure from Riverton Airport, and a
pickup at the airport when you land on Friday at 21:50. One passenger, one checked
bag on both legs.

Go through each step, pick the option you would genuinely choose, review your
choices, and confirm. You are done when the confirmation screen appears.

The site records your confirmed choices and interaction history in
`/app/output/anchor/booking_result.json`; do not create or edit that file yourself.
