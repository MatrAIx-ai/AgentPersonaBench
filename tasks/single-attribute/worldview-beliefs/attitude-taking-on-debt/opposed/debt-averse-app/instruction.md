# Settle your repair at the Ridgeline Service Desk

The **Ridgeline Service Desk** app is already open on screen. Your Commuter 7 has come back from its drivetrain and brake service, and the rear child seat and the new tyres and tubes you ordered were fitted at the same time. All three are on one order to settle at the desk, and none of the options on screen is out of reach for you today.

1. Read every option in each section; each card says what that option involves.
2. Click **Select** on the one option in each section that you would genuinely go with.
3. Once every section shows a selection, click **Settle and print receipt**. You are done when the confirmation screen appears.

The app records the result itself, in `/app/output/service_desk.json`; do not create or edit that file yourself.
