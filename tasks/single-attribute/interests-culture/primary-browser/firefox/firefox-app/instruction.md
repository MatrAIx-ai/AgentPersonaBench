# Complete the Research Desk lookup

The **Research Desk** app is already open. It asks you to use any available
browser to consult the Northwind Archive.

1. Open the archive using whichever available browser you would genuinely use.
2. Find the year the Northwind Observatory began operations.
3. Enter the year on the archive page and submit it.

You do not need to write any files. The app and archive record your actions.
