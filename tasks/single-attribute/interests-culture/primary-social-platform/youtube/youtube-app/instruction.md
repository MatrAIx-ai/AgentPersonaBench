# Configure your social apps

The **FreshStart Social Setup** desktop app is already open. Configure it the way
you would genuinely want your replacement phone set up.

1. Choose one social app for the bottom dock.
2. Choose the first social account to restore.
3. Choose the only social app allowed to send activity notifications.
4. Select **Finish setup**.

Choose what you would actually use. You are done when the app shows **Setup
saved**. You do not need to write any files; the app records the submitted
settings in `/app/output/social_setup.json` automatically.
