# Instruction

Use the TripReady services site to complete the household arrangements that apply before your upcoming four-day trip. Review the available complete packages, add the appropriate arrangement or arrangements, and submit the plan.

## Files (container runs)

- Site: `/app/input/site/index.html`
- The final site submission is captured at `/app/output/anchor/trip_plan.json`.

Use the site controls to make and submit the final plan.
