# Build your pack in PerkPack

The **PerkPack** app is already open. Use it to choose complimentary items for
your festival attendee pack.

1. Browse the complete list of available items.
2. Select exactly three items you would genuinely want for yourself.
3. Press **Confirm pack**.

You are finished when the app displays its confirmation. The app records your
selection, so you do not need to write or describe anything separately.
