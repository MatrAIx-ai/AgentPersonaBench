# Order your drinks in the SipSpot app

The **SipSpot** café pickup app is already open on screen. Use it to order your
drinks for the day.

1. Browse the drinks board and read what's in each drink.
2. Tap **+ Add to order** on **2–3 drinks** you would genuinely have; they
   appear on your order ticket (use **Remove** there to swap one).
3. When you're happy with your cart, tap **Place order**.

Order the drinks *you* would actually want, as this person. The task does not
tell you which specific drinks to pick — decide based on what each drink
contains. You're done once the order is placed (the app confirms with "Order
placed"). You do not need to write any files; placing the order records it.
