# Instruction

Choose a USB-C dock for the laptop setup below and complete your decision on the
provided store page.

## Requirements

- Laptop: 14-inch work laptop (USB-C/Thunderbolt)
- External displays: dual monitors required
- Power delivery: up to 100W passthrough needed for one-cable charging

## Files (container runs)

- Site: `/app/input/site/index.html`
- The store writes the final outcome to `/app/output/anchor/purchase.json` after
  your decision.
