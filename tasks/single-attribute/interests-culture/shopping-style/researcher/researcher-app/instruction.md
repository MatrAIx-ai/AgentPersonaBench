# Buy a Washer in WasherMart

The **WasherMart** desktop app is already open. You need to decide on one washer
for this home setup:

- Alcove size limit: 27.0 in width, 32.0 in depth, 38.5 in height
- Doorway clearance: 29.0 in minimum pass-through width
- Power: must run on standard 120V household outlet

What to do:

1. Review candidates in the app.
2. Choose the washer you want for this setup.
3. Complete your decision with **Confirm purchase** or **Defer purchase**.

No files are required from you; the app will write the final artifact to `/app/output/order.json` after your in-app decision.
