# Instruction

The **StudioBook** app (Riverside Makerspace's booking kiosk) is already open on
screen. Use it to reserve your own recurring weekly session for the semester.

1. Read the list of session times — this is the slot you will keep all semester.
2. Click **exactly one** session time, plus any add-ons you actually want.
3. Click **Confirm booking**. You're done once the app confirms.

Decide based on what each option is. You do not need to write any files; the app
records your booking itself.
