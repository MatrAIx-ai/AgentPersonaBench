# Book your follow-up consultation

Use the Meridian Clinic patient portal to book your own follow-up consultation.
The portal asks for four settings: how the consultation itself runs, how your
results come back to you, how the nurse takes you through the preparation, and
how the reminder before the day reaches you.

Go through each step, pick the option you would genuinely choose, review your
choices, and confirm. You are done when the confirmation screen appears.

The site records your confirmed choices and interaction history in
`/app/output/anchor/booking_result.json`; do not create or edit that file yourself.
