# Book your trip in TrackPass

The **TrackPass** rail-booking app is already open on screen, with your outbound
journey found. Use it to book the tickets you would genuinely buy.

1. Read each section — ticket, fare type, class and seat — and what every option
   covers and costs.
2. Click **Select** on one option in each of the four sections.
3. The optional extras are yours to take or leave.
4. When you are happy with the booking, click **Book tickets**.

The app records the booking itself; there is no file for you to write. Just book
the trip — no explanation.
