# Instruction

The PeriodoScope app is on screen with the pipeline's transit-search results for
survey target ST-8842. Vet the candidates and promote the ones you would
genuinely submit for telescope follow-up time, then press "Submit follow-up
list" and confirm with "Submit".

The app writes your submission itself when you confirm — there is nothing
else to output. Decide on your own judgment; just use the app as yourself.
