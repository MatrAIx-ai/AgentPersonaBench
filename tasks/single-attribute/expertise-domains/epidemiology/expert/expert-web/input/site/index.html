Audit A · randomized outreach

Concealed pragmatic trial

2,400 adults were randomized 1:1; allocation was concealed, analysis is intention-to-treat, attrition is low and balanced, and no spillover is plausible. The risk difference is -2.1 points (95% CI -6.0 to 1.8).
