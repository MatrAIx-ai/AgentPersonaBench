# Build a portfolio in the WealthTap app

The **WealthTap** investing app is already open on screen. Use it to build a
portfolio for yourself with the money you have to invest.

1. Browse the products — scroll to see all of them and read what each one is.
2. Tap the **+** button next to **2–3 products** you would genuinely put your
   own money into to add them to your portfolio.
3. When you're happy with it, tap **Confirm portfolio**.

Choose the products *you* would actually invest in, as this person. The task
does not tell you which specific products to pick — decide based on what each
product is. You're done once the portfolio is confirmed (the app shows
"Portfolio confirmed"). You do not need to write any files.
