# Complete the BidDesk Opportunity Portfolio

The **BidDesk Opportunity Workspace** desktop app is already open. Review all 16
client opportunities. For each one, choose the compensation package and delivery
commitment you would genuinely accept, then select **Save & next**. After all 16
proposals are saved, select **Submit portfolio**.

No files are required from you. The app records the completed plan in
`/app/output/proposal_portfolio.json` after submission.
