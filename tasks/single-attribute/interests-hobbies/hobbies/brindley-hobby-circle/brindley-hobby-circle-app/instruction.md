# Instruction

The Brindley Hobby Circle app is already open. Book your six months in it. For each month in the
month strip along the top, open it, read its four options and click the button on the one you want. Some months
already hold a booking marked Booked for you. Any booking can be changed, and a month counts only
after you click one of its four buttons, including on a booking you keep.

When all six months show a tick, click Save. The app writes `/app/output/order_result.json` itself
when you save. Just complete the bookings; no explanation is needed.
