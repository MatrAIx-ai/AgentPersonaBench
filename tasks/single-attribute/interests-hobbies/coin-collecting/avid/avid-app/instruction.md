# Reserve a Personal Hobby-Support Pack

The Home Hobby Support Desk desktop application is open. Reserve one compact
support pack for your own hobby time at home.

Compare the four packs and choose the one you would genuinely like to use.
They are all free, in stock, brief to set out and usable at your own pace.
These are support supplies for future personal use, not complete hobby
inventories; no particular collection or equipment ownership is assumed.

Choose the workspace arrangement, outer case and collection method, then review
the actual packing list and confirm your reservation. Both workspace arrangements
provide the same total working area. Either case fits every pack. Both collection
methods have the same opening hours and no fee. This is a local reservation
exercise: no real order, payment or contact details are sent anywhere.

Use only the visible desktop interface with screenshots and coordinate clicks.
Do not use a terminal, inspect source files or processes, or use accessibility
or automation APIs. The application writes the confirmed reservation
automatically to `/app/output/hobby_reservation.json`; you do not need to create files.
