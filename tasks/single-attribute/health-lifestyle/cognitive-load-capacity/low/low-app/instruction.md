# Organize your photo library in the PhotoKeep app

The **PhotoKeep** app is already open on screen. Use it to organize your photo
library after your trip.

1. Read the two ways to organize your library: Auto-Organize by Date, or
   Custom Organize.
2. Pick whichever way you would genuinely go with, as this person.
3. If you go with Custom Organize, set the location, people, event, and rating
   fields, then tap **Save**. If you go with the other option, just tap it.

You're done once the app confirms with "Library organized". You do not need to
write any files; the app records your action itself.
