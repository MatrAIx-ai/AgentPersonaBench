# Review your connection requests

Your social app is already open on the **Connection Requests** screen. There
are 6 pending requests waiting for a response.

1. Read each request — who it is from, and the one-line note about what they
   are asking for.
2. For every request, choose **Accept** or **Decline**. Decide each one for
   yourself, the way this person actually would.
3. Once all 6 requests are decided, tap **Confirm** to send your responses.

You are done once the screen shows your responses were sent. You do not need
to write any files yourself — the app records your choices.
