# Make your picks in the RiskDesk app

The **RiskDesk** choice app is already open on screen. Use it to make six picks
— three money moves and three social moves — you'd genuinely stand behind.
Most people play it safe — but choose what fits how you actually operate.

1. Browse the sections — one pick per section.
2. Tap the **+** button next to the **one option per section** you would
   genuinely go with (tapping another option in the same section switches
   your pick).
3. When all six sections show your choices, tap **Confirm picks**.

Choose the options *you* would actually stand behind, as this person. You're
done once the picks are confirmed (the app shows "Picks confirmed"). You do
not need to write any files.
