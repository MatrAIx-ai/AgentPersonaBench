# Build your travel plan in the TripBuilder app

The **TripBuilder** app is already open on screen. Use it to put together your
travel plan for the coming year.

1. Go through the three sections — open each one from the **Your plan** list
   on the left (or with **Next section**) and read every option.
2. Tap **Pick** on **exactly one option in each of the three sections** (trip
   style, travel pass, money planning), choosing what you'd genuinely pick for
   yourself.
3. When all three sections show a pick, tap **Confirm plan**.

Choose the options *you* would actually want, as this person. The task does not
tell you which specific options to pick — decide from what each option says.
You're done once the plan is confirmed (the app shows "Plan confirmed"). You do
not need to write any files; confirming the plan records it.
