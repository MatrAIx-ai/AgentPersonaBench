# Build your two-evening pass in CulturePass

The **CulturePass** app is already open. Use it to choose one Friday experience
and one Saturday experience that you would genuinely enjoy most.

1. Browse every option for both evenings.
2. Select exactly one option in each section.
3. Press **Confirm pass**.

You are finished when the app displays its confirmation. The app records the
itinerary, so you do not need to write or describe anything separately.
