How a Saturday works

Coaches leave from the depot at the time shown on the board and return by early evening. Your $24 covers the activity, the coach both ways, and any food listed on the detail sheet.

One reservation per member each Saturday.

At the depot

  • Check in 15 minutes before departure
  • Bring a water bottle
  • Show your ticket on your phone

Help desk

Questions about access, food or timing? The desk is staffed Friday 9–5 and at the depot on Saturday morning.
