# Reserve your analysis and briefing work

The **BriefingPlanner** app is already open on screen. Choose two complete packages you would genuinely lead.

1. Browse the options and read what each one is.
2. Tap **+ Reserve** on **exactly 2 options** you would genuinely lead (tapping
   **✓ Reserved** again, or **Remove** in Your rota, takes back one you didn't mean).
3. When you're happy with your two picks, tap **Reserve packages**.

Decide from what each option is, as this person — the task does not tell you
which to pick. You're done once the app confirms with "Packages reserved".
You do not need to write any files; submitting in the app records it.
