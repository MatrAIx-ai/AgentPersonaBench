Food room

Senior community center

Four open roles. Open each one to read its responsibilities, then choose the one you would accept.

Participant Host

Senior community center

Keep the session welcoming without preparing food.

Check attendance, distribute printed recipe cards, point out handwashing stations, and direct every food-preparation question to an instructor.

First-Shift Kitchen Assistant

Senior community center

Learn kitchen basics while supporting the instructor.

Learn safe grip and measuring basics from the instructor, portion labeled ingredients, replenish workstations from a checklist, and follow step-by-step directions.

Culinary Technique Coach

Senior community center

Execute a prepared menu and coach one table with expert backup nearby.

Execute a prepared savory menu, demonstrate knife and stovetop techniques at one table, monitor doneness, and handle routine substitutions. The program chef owns complex issues.

Large-Event Culinary Systems Specialist

Senior community center

Requires prior independent delivery of a 200-guest multi-course service with simultaneous severe-allergy plans.

Create the menu without provided recipes, validate simultaneous severe-allergy controls, and diagnose complex sauce, sugar, and protein failures across four stations.

Baking room

Neighborhood youth center

Four open roles. Open each one to read its responsibilities, then choose the one you would accept.

Dough Technique Assistant

Neighborhood youth center

Use prior bread-making experience to provide hands-on guidance.

Independently answer questions about dough texture, correct mixing and shaping, identify under-kneading, and help learners adjust hydration while the lead manages the ovens.

Materials-Desk Assistant

Neighborhood youth center

Keep essential paper supplies ready without baking duties.

Label aprons and printed guides, restock paper supplies from a checklist, and collect forms. Baking staff handle all ingredients, ovens, demonstrations, and technique questions.

Bread Workshop Lead

Neighborhood youth center

Teach and troubleshoot the complete bread-making session.

Demonstrate gluten development, judge fermentation, control proofing and oven timing, diagnose failed batches, and adapt the formula to room temperature during class.

Recipe-Step Baking Assistant

Neighborhood youth center

Use basic hands-on baking familiarity to carry out a provided cookie recipe.

Combine premeasured ingredients by the provided recipe, scoop equal portions, and rotate labeled trays on a timer. The lead checks texture, doneness, and any batch problems.

Media room

Community school

Four open roles. Open each one to read its responsibilities, then choose the one you would accept.

Event Studio Operator

Community school

Run a technical studio and deliver individual critiques.

Design multi-light setups, calculate flash ratios, tether and color-calibrate captures, troubleshoot mixed lighting, and give detailed technical critiques without another instructor present.

Guided Portrait Assistant

Community school

Adjust a prepared portrait setup with the lead photographer nearby.

Adjust shutter speed, aperture, and ISO from a reference card, position a reflector from a floor mark, check focus, and refer lighting or equipment problems to the lead photographer.

Event-Story Curator

Community school

Build the participant story display without taking photographs.

Interview participants, organize consent forms, select submitted quotes, and assemble display captions. Instructors handle all photography and camera questions.

Phone-Photo Documentation Assistant

Community school

Take simple activity photos from a prepared shot list.

Follow a prepared shot list, use framing, tap-to-focus, and steady-hand basics on a phone, and send every image to the lead photographer for review.
