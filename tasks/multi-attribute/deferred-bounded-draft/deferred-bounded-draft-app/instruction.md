# Choose lessons in the GuidePicker app

The **GuidePicker** app is already open on screen. Your onboarding plan needs two client lessons — use the app to select them.

Do not optimize for a generic client lead; choose what fits your own established work-timing and revision habits.

1. Browse the options and read what each one is.
2. Tap the **+** button next to **exactly 2 options** you'd genuinely send (tapping
   again removes one you didn't mean).
3. When you're happy with your two picks, tap **Save lessons**.

Decide from what each option is, as this person — the task does not tell you
which to pick. You're done once the app confirms with "Lessons saved".
You do not need to write any files; submitting in the app records it.
